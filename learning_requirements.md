# Learning Requirements

### Backend Development
* **Build Great APIs:** Design, develop, and maintain scalable, robust backend features and APIs using modern frameworks, demonstrating proficiency in Golang.
* **Keep Systems Reliable:** Ensure high performance and reliability across distributed systems, backed by a strong understanding of backend fundamentals, distributed architectures, messaging systems, and caching.
* **Power Core Platforms:** Contribute directly to systems powering authentication, billing, AI tooling, observability, connectors, and developer experience.

### System Architecture
* **Design Smart Architecture:** Design and implement efficient, secure, and scalable architectures that support fast-growing products, utilizing a strong understanding of databases.
* **Optimize Infrastructure:** Collaborate with infrastructure teams on deployment, monitoring, and performance optimization, leveraging experience with both cloud-based (AWS, GCP, Azure) and bare-metal infrastructure.
